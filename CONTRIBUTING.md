# Contributing to AI Magic

The easiest way to help is to **suggest a source**: open an issue with the
[Suggest a source](../../issues/new?template=suggest-a-source.yml) form.

Good suggestions are:

- **recent** - about AI as it is now, not five years ago;
- **specific** - one idea that can be drawn, not a whole field;
- **checkable** - a link, plus a timestamp or section where the idea is said.

Spotted a mistake in a lesson, a wrong timestamp or a broken link? Open an issue
or a pull request against the day note in [days/](days/). Corrections are
credited in the note.

Everything here is MIT-licensed, so the cards and notes can be reused in your own
talks, wikis and lessons. A link back is appreciated.
